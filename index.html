Why AI-KIDO

AI is powerful. Without a method, it is unpredictable.

Used on the fly, language models forget, contradict themselves and leave each developer with their own habits. AI-KIDO replaces improvisation with a shared, durable way of working.

  1. 01

    It forgets

    Every session starts from zero. Your assistant gets a persistent memory it navigates from a single entry point, so context is never re-explained.

  2. 02

    It contradicts itself

    Truth tables give every fact one home. When sources disagree, the authoritative one is known and the gap is caught early.

  3. 03

    It is a solo sport

    A common language, defined roles and shared workflows let a whole team collaborate with AI the same way.

  4. 04

    It does not last

    Internal agents validate, rebalance and index your knowledge, so the Memory Bank keeps pace with the project.

How it works

Three ideas, applied consistently.

Together they turn scattered notes into a knowledge base that both humans and AI can trust.

01

Structure your knowledge

One tree, one entry point. Knowledge is organized by concepts, techniques, personas and use cases, so an assistant finds exactly what it needs and nothing more.

02

Decide what stays open and what is settled

Truth tables stay flexible and evolve with the project. Hard points lock the decisions you have validated and always win when there is a conflict.

03

Let agents keep it healthy

Internal agents search, check compliance, resolve conflicts, fill gaps and update the base, while preserving your project's own context.

A Memory Bank that maintains itself

One entry point. Agents that keep it healthy.

The single entry point is prompts/index.md. Hard points (hard_points) are authoritative, followed by the Truth Tables (gen).

prompts/
├── index.md            # single entry point
├── hard_points/        # immutable truths (absolute priority)
│   ├── agents/  commands/  system/  ...
└── gen/                # project Truth Tables
    ├── concepts/   techniques/
    ├── agents/     personas/
    └── use_cases/
  • Explore Bank / Code / AllSearch the documentation, the code, or both in parallel.
  • Truth EvaluatorDetermines the authoritative source when sources conflict.
  • Validator & Doc HeaderChecks file compliance and manages document headers.
  • Balancer & IndexRebalances the tree and keeps indexes up to date.
  • Bank Populate & RACI DelegatorFills the gaps and classifies new documents.
  • Fusion & Update ControllerUpdates AI-KIDO while preserving the project's local context.
